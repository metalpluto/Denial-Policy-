Timely Filing Policy

All claims must be submitted within 120 days of the date of service unless
a payer-specific contract states otherwise. Claims received after this
window will be denied for untimely filing and are not eligible for
standard appeal unless the provider can demonstrate a qualifying delay
outside their control.

Qualifying exceptions to the 120-day window include: retroactive patient
eligibility determinations, coordination-of-benefits delays where a
primary payer's determination was pending, and documented system outages
at the clearinghouse or payer during the filing window.

To contest a timely filing denial, the provider must submit proof of an
earlier, timely submission attempt (such as a clearinghouse acceptance
report or certified mail receipt) or documentation of a qualifying delay.
Claims resubmitted purely due to internal processing backlog do not
qualify for an exception.

Secondary claims follow a separate 90-day window measured from the date
of the primary payer's explanation of benefits (EOB), not the original
date of service.
