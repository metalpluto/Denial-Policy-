Prior Authorization Policy

Certain procedures require prior authorization from the payer before the
service is rendered. The current prior authorization list includes
advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET), non-emergency inpatient admissions,
select durable medical equipment, and specialty pharmacy infusions.

A claim will be denied for missing authorization when the procedure code
billed appears on the payer's prior authorization list and no
authorization number is on file at the time the claim is processed. This
denial applies regardless of whether the service was clinically
appropriate.

Retroactive authorization requests are permitted only in the following
circumstances: emergency services where obtaining prior authorization
was not feasible before treatment, or documented payer system errors
that prevented submission of the request in advance.

To appeal a missing authorization denial, the provider must submit a
retroactive authorization request along with clinical documentation
supporting medical necessity, and, where applicable, evidence of the
emergency circumstances that prevented advance authorization.

Providers are responsible for verifying authorization requirements before
scheduling any procedure on the prior authorization list; the patient's
insurance card or the payer's provider portal should be checked before
each date of service, as requirements can change.
